## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Blue Space handles personal data across the website, marketplace, seller storefronts, service booking links, seller and provider dashboards, order and quote tools, account verification, payment or settlement, payouts, support, and analytics. It applies together with the Terms and Conditions.

## 2. Operator

Blue Space is operating under the trade name “Business registration pending; Blue Space” until formal registration is completed. Registered legal name and Ghana physical address details will be inserted once confirmed.

## 3. Personal data collected

Depending on use, we may collect account details, names, business names, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords or authentication information, profile pictures, store or service information, product images and listings, customer names and delivery locations, order and quote details, payment and payout information, verification status, support messages, and technical or usage data.

We do not expose private hostel room numbers, live GPS coordinates, or device IMEIs to users. Blue Space users must be at least 18 years old.

## 4. Purposes of processing

Blue Space processes personal data to create and manage accounts, operate storefronts and booking links, verify users and payout recipients, process or facilitate transactions and withdrawals, maintain delivery-confirmed settlement records, prevent fraud and circumvention, manage refunds and disputes, provide support, improve service reliability, analyse platform use, and comply with Ghanaian legal obligations.

Processing is based on contract, legal obligation, consent where applicable, security needs, or Blue Space's legitimate interests that do not override user rights.

## 5. Payment and settlement data

Moolre receives and processes payment, payout, identity or recipient verification, and transaction information necessary to operate checkout and settlement. Blue Space maintains related order, status, refund, commission, and payout records.

Personal data is not transferred into a seller's personal account as a condition of payment. Blue Space does not present delivery-confirmed settlement as a bank escrow or trust account.

## 6. Analytics

Blue Space may use PostHog for page views, click and feature usage, page-leave events, frontend exception capture, and optional session activity. Form inputs are masked and selected text may be masked. Query strings and URL fragments are removed from recorded URL properties where configured.

## 7. Marketing and service communications

Blue Space may send marketing email, SMS, or in-app messages. Users may opt out of marketing through the relevant message instructions or by contacting support.

Transaction, verification, delivery, refund, security, and dispute messages may still be sent where necessary to operate or protect the service.

## 8. Cookies and local storage

Blue Space may use browser storage and necessary provider technologies for authentication, security, analytics, and service operation. A separate optional cookie-consent program is not represented unless clearly implemented and activated.

## 9. Service providers

We may share relevant data with Moolre, Render, Vercel, Namecheap, Supabase, Cloudinary, PostHog, professional advisers, mediators, legal or government authorities, and providers or acquirers where needed and lawful.

Blue Space does not sell personal data in the ordinary sense. Provider transfers must support the platform, security, compliance, dispute, or transaction purpose.

## 10. International processing

Some providers may process or store data outside Ghana. Blue Space uses reasonable contractual and technical safeguards where required and processes data in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

## 11. Security

Blue Space uses administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards intended to protect personal data. Staff and campus onboarders must not ask for passwords, Mobile Money PINs, SMS OTPs, private hostel room numbers, or device IMEIs.

No system is completely secure. Users must protect credentials and report suspected account compromise through support.

## 12. Retention

Blue Space retains account and order records for up to seven years from the latest relevant transaction or account closure, or longer where required for law, taxation, accounting, fraud, security, litigation, or dispute resolution.

Active records may remain for the 48-hour confirmation window, a live dispute, an appeal, a refund, or a payout review.

## 13. User rights

Subject to Ghanaian law and applicable exceptions, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection, information about relevant recipients, withdrawal of consent, or a usable copy of applicable personal data.

Blue Space may verify identity before responding and may retain data where a legal exception or live dispute applies.

You may contact the Ghana Data Protection Commission where a data protection issue falls within its jurisdiction.

## 14. Account closure

Users may request account closure through support. Closure may deactivate access and storefront functions, while required records remain retained for legal, tax, security, fraud, accounting, or dispute purposes.

## 15. Seller and provider customer data

Sellers and providers may use buyer names, phone numbers, delivery locations, order notes, quotes, and service evidence only to fulfil, support, and resolve an order. They must not publish, sell, misuse, or use the data for unrelated marketing. They must retain it only as reasonably necessary.

## 16. Data minimisation and privacy practices

Blue Space does not expose private hostel room numbers, live GPS coordinates, or phone IMEI values to other users. Off-platform transactions do not receive Blue Space support or data-processing protections.

## 17. Changes

Blue Space may update this Policy for legal, security, provider, operational, or product changes. The effective date is the launch date until otherwise published.
